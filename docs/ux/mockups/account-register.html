On the phone

  • Accounts: the list of accounts with their balances; tap the current account to open its register.
  • Register: transactions grouped by day, search and filters; the circle on the right marks a transaction as cleared, tapping the row edits it full screen.
  • Quick entry from the central button: amount, place (which proposes the category used last time), category with its available amount, account, Save.
  • Everything works offline too: changes sync later.
