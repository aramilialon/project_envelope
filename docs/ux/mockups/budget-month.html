On the phone

  • Only the available amount per category: tap a category to see its detail, tap a group's name to collapse or expand it.
  • The central button opens quick expense entry, in three taps.
  • Groups with overspending move to the top, so the problem is visible at once.
  • "Assign" opens the same form as the desktop, full screen: from unassigned money to a category, for this month or a future one.
  • A category's detail lists its scheduled transactions, with Record and Skip, and the month's assignments, each with Undo.
  • Same data as the desktop: covering an overspend there changes it here too.
